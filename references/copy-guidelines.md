# Copy & Compliance Guidelines (v0 framework · extended over time)

> Status: baseline, distilled from the live site. Add new rules directly under the matching heading.

## Brand usage

- Brand word in headings, fixed form: `<b>Harvok®</b>` — the ® is mandatory and the brand word is bold (see every section heading on the live site)
- In body copy, first mention is Harvok®, later mentions may be Harvok
- Fixed product/technology spellings: Monocoque Uni-Body, 48V Electric Power System, Aluminium Monocoque Body (Australian spelling: Aluminium)

## Tone of voice

- Confident, engineering-led: claims are backed by concrete mechanisms ("48V all-electric system — safer, quieter, powerful enough to run everyday appliances without caravan-park hookups"), never stacked adjectives
- Speaks to Australian family travel: off-grid, towing, road trips
- Headline patterns: declarative sentence with bold brand word ("Harvok® Changing the Way the World Thinks About Caravanning."); CTAs start with a verb ("Secure Your 2026 Harvok Sooner")

## Structural conventions

- Eyebrow labels: 2–3 words, uppercase ("TOP STORY" / "HARVOK HIGHLIGHTS")
- Feature lists use `<ul><li>`, 3–7 words per item
- Button copy: "More about …" / "View On Google" pattern, title case
